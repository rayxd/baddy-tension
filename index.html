    How it works & tips

    The tight string bed rings like a drum skin. Its pitch goes up with the square root of tension, so the app measures the pitch of your tap and works backwards using the racket's head size and string gauge.

    • Tap the centre of the strings. A fingernail flick or the butt of another racket works well.
    • Measure somewhere quiet, with the phone 5–20 cm from the strings.
    • Press Calibrate… right after a fresh restring, entering the tension it was strung at. This corrects for your racket's frame and string, and makes later readings far more accurate.
    • Expect about 5–10% loss in the first day after stringing. That's normal.
    • Using the same racket over time, the trend is more reliable than any single number.

    Racket

    Measure the inside of the string bed from the top of the head to the throat (length) and side to side (width). Typical badminton: 220 × 190 mm. Common gauges: BG65 0.70, BG80 0.68, Exbolt 63 0.63.
